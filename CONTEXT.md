# Domain glossary

- **Flexible week**: a repeatable training template (play, tactics, analysis, openings, threat awareness and endgame), without calendar deadlines or streaks. Session counts derive from existing logs; external play/endgame checkoffs and an explicit next-week boundary use optional settings. Gaps never reset the template. Game Review completion and trends derive from the disposable review cache, so they can be rebuilt by reviewing games again.

- **Learner**: the single user of the app.
- **Position key**: the normalised identity of a position (first four FEN fields, with the en-passant square only when the capture is legal). It is produced by `positionKey()` in the Game module. See ADR-0001.
- **Repertoire**: the learner's chosen openings, stored as a graph of position keys connected by UCI moves. White plays the Italian and the London. Black plays the Caro-Kann and the QGD/...d5 setup.
- **Line**: a named move path through the repertoire graph, used for learning order and display. A line is not an identity.
- **Our move / Reply**: a repertoire edge played by the learner, or by the opponent.
- **Card**: one schedulable unit of practice with a content-derived ID (`rep:`, `mistake:`, `puzzle:`); non-scheduling rule quiz identities use `rule:<ruleId>:example-v1`. See ADR-0002.
- **Review log**: the append-only record of every attempt on a card. SRS state is derived from it.
- **Practice stage**: the middle stage per side and line, after explicit Learn completion and before one clean whole-line recall unlocks Drill. It hides ideas, arrows and Hint; mistakes reveal the correct move and idea for retry and require a new clean run. Stage comes only from the review log, survives backup, and Practice never schedules cards. Existing scheduled lines stay in Drill; a shared position does not promote other lines. See ADR-0002.
- **Learn mode / Drill mode**: in learn mode the learner walks through a line with explanations. In drill mode the learner recalls our moves while the app plays the replies.
- **Golden rule**: a catalogued chess principle with an ID, a phase, a short form, a reason, an exception and a source creator.
- **Threat Scan**: an auto-graded exercise. The learner marks legal checks/captures as arrows for both sides and hanging pieces as separate square marks. Checking captures and promotion variants share one arrow. For the side not to move, the generator flips the turn and clears en passant; it skips that move list when the original side is in check (passing would leave the other king already attacked). A piece is hanging when an opponent has a legal capture and either no legal recapture exists after that capture or the cheapest legal capturer is worth less than the victim. Pinned attackers and illegal pinned/king recaptures do not count. En passant marks the captured pawn's square; promotion captures share one arrow. Kings themselves are never hanging pieces. Normal repertoire scans exclude positions where the side to move is in check.
- **What changed?**: a 30-second Threat Scan after an animated opponent move. Required marks are only new legal checks and captures by the side that just moved, plus the learner's newly hanging pieces, compared before/after by drawable mark identity. The responding learner's opportunities never count. Comparisons with the side to move in check at either endpoint are excluded; only positions with 1-12 new marks are used. The countdown starts when the move is revealed and submits once at expiry.
- **Safety Check**: an optional prompt before submitting a move ("Is my move safe? What does the opponent threaten?"). It is ungraded. In opening Drill, an entered move is previewed before judgment with Continue/Take back. When disabled, the prompt appears once per drill session after the first failed attempt. Learn and whole-line Practice are uninterrupted.
- **Woodpecker cycle**: one pass over a persisted fixed puzzle set, selected from a motif and rating range. Each cycle aims to beat the previous cycle's sum of attempt times, with clean-solve accuracy shown separately. A first Hint ends an attempt as a failure; the learner may continue exploring the solution. Cycles and their times come from the review log, never change SRS intervals and are never rated.
- **Game Review**: post-game engine analysis of a whole game: classifications, accuracy, eval graph and explanations.
- **Hint**: on-demand help in steps. Step 1 names the motif or the piece to look at, step 2 shows the move.
- **Finding**: a structured, evidence-backed fact from the Explainer (motif, squares, proving line), rendered as text.
- **Classification**: the Chess.com-style label of a move (Book … Blunder). See ADR-0004.
- **Deviation**: the first ply in a game that leaves the repertoire. It is either our deviation, an unknown reply, or the line's normal end.

- **Cohort**: per side, a starting/target set of 6 new lines (setting 3-12). Authored main lines come first, then the weight of the first branching opponent reply, then line ID. Practice/Drill progress and manual unlocks remain active even beyond the target or after lowering it. A cohort whose every card has interval >= 3 days and no repertoire lapse in the last 7 days admits at most one next line per evaluation and side. Manual unlocks are non-scheduling Learn-log entries (`lineUnlocked`), never lesson completions or discoveries. Active lines are ordered by total card lapses descending, then minimum ease ascending, with the reason visible.
- **Coverage**: unique trainable positions per opening/side or line: discovered by viewing them in Learn, practised by a Practice/Drill attempt, in Drill by membership in any Drill-stage line (OR rule), mastered at an SRS interval >= 21 days. Only legacy Learn completions without the `discoveryMode` marker discover the full line. New Learn entries carry `discoveryMode: "visits"`; completing a lesson never discovers skipped positions. Shared positions count once per scope; position coverage never promotes a shared line. All counters derive from graph + review log.

- **Golden Rules quiz**: two-choice recognition on authored examples. Results use non-scheduling review-log entries; successful answers supplement the learned-rule state, with later manual unlearn marks respected.

- **Sync code**: a private 32-byte random seed stored only in the local secrets store. It derives independent encryption/authentication keys for an optional cloud namespace; the code never enters backups. Sync always merges and leaves the manual import Undo point intact. See ADR-0005.
